# Other math, same generator

## Polynomial

$$
a x^2 + b x + c
$$

## Piecewise

$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 & x > 0 \\
-x & x < 0 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

## Sum squares

```json
{"inputs":{"x":"list<real>"}}
```

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\operatorname{Length}(x)} x_i^2
$$

## Within limits

$$
f \le d \land u \ge r
$$
